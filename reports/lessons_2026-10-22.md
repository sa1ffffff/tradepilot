# Lessons from the demo run of 2026-10-20 → 22

What the first live demo run taught us, and what changed because of it. Each lesson
names its evidence. Read this before the next research round or demo run.

Run: 46 h, 13 trades, +117.78 USDT (spot −29.02 over 10, futures +146.80 over 3).
Details: `demo_run_2026-10-22.md`.

## 1. The backtest is a faithful model of the bot: trust research over short runs
- Evidence: `session_report.py` matched **10 of 10** spot entries to the backtest over the
  same 45.5 h (no extra or missing trades); entry prices within −12.5 to +4.1 bps.
- So: judge strategies by research (months of out-of-sample data), not by a day or two of
  demo P&L. 13 trades say almost nothing about profitability.

## 2. Spot limit orders fill less often than assumed → priced in
- Evidence: 54% of spot entries and non-stop exits filled as maker (8 of 15), vs 70% assumed.
  Futures filled 6 of 6, too few to rely on.
- Changed: `MAKER_FILL_RATE=0.54` in `.env` (from 0.7), so research and backtests now use it.
- Effect: the two spot candidates still pass (BTC 4h chandelier_trend +22.9%,
  ETH 1h ema_cross_adx +24.1% out of sample; `research_20261022_175843_maker.md`).
  Futures results are insensitive to it (70% vs 54% nearly identical, because swap
  fees are small; `research_20261022_180711_maker_swap.md` and `..._181520_maker_swap.md`).

## 3. The day's big win was luck, not a "solid signal"
- Evidence: the BTC 5m test slot's +158.26 USDT short was one of 2 trades out of 712 (90 days)
  that made ~10R. Over those 712 trades the strategy averaged −0.64R; none of 7 entry
  traits (ADX, 16 h / 2-day trend agreement, volatility, combinations) separated winners
  from losers.
- So: don't size up on "strong-looking" signals without a tested filter. The trailing stop
  is what kept the win (see 4). The 5m test slot is for exercising the plumbing only.

## 4. A trailing stop protects outsized winners; a tight one hurts trend strategies
- Evidence: tight 1R/1× trailing cut the 8 research strategies' 3-year returns from 568%
  to 391%; 2R/2× left them at 567% with lower drawdowns. It improved the BTC 5m slot
  (−41% → −32% over 30 days).
- Changed: trailing stop added live and in the backtest; defaults 2R/2×, BTC test slot 1R/1×.

## 5. With trailing included, two current futures slots no longer pass
- Evidence: in the 2026-10-22 swap research (3 years, walk-forward, trailing on),
  ETH 1h ema_cross_adx and SOL 4h ema_cross_adx are not CANDIDATEs. The other six
  (ADA 4h supertrend, AVAX 4h supertrend, BNB 4h donchian_breakout, DOGE 4h supertrend,
  LTC 4h bollinger_reversion, XRP 4h donchian_breakout) still are.
- Suggested: replace SOL with SOL 4h donchian_breakout (PF 1.53, +29% out of sample,
  3 of 4 folds positive), and ETH with ETH 4h trend_pullback (PF 1.34, +13.5%) or drop ETH. Not applied yet:
  it changes what trades, so it's the user's call.
- Caution: dozens of strategy × market combinations were tested, so some passes are luck.
  The "auto" rows (choosing among all strategies each fold) pass on only 4–5 of 18 swap
  markets, and on none in spot.

## 6. OKX demo is flaky; the bots cope
- Evidence: 17 spot errors and 64 futures network warnings in 46 h, including bursts of
  "system busy" (50013). Every one was recovered from with no lost or duplicated trade.
- Changed earlier in the run: a lock stops two copies of a bot running at once (two spot
  bots had been running in parallel).
