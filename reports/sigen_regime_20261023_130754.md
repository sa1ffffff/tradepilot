## Decision (2026-10-23)

**The filter stays off.** It failed all three checks fixed before the test. Even on the older
trades it was set on, the signals it held back were profitable on average (+0.23R): the filter
raised the average by dropping smaller winners, and cut total R from +482 to +296. On the newer
trades, the held-back signals were the better ones (+0.23R against +0.12R kept). Breakouts out
of quiet, choppy markets are where large trends start, and a chop filter removes exactly those.

On the 30-day replay the same rule would have turned -29R into -10R. That is the trap this test
avoids: a month of one regime makes any filter tuned to it look good.

**Shadow trial instead.** Across all 28 rules, the 30-candle efficiency ratio below 0.20 was the
only one that helped both before the split (+0.012R) and after it (+0.093R). Because it was
picked after seeing both periods, it can't be trusted from history. SiGen now tags every live
trend signal it would hold back, without blocking it, and the Signal history page compares the
tagged signals with the rest. It can be switched on only once at least 60 closed live trend
signals on each side show a clear gain.
